Applications

Where titanium earns its cost — the industries, grades and product forms we supply most often, and what each application actually requires.

Chemical processing

Heat exchangers, reactor vessels, columns and piping in chloride and oxidising service. Grade 2 for general duty; Grade 7 (Ti-0.15Pd) where reducing acids attack the metal.

Gr.2 · Gr.7

Desalination and marine

Tube bundles, tube sheets and waterbox linings for multi-stage flash and reverse-osmosis plants. Seawater resistance and a low-fouling surface matter more here than high strength.

Gr.2 · ASTM B338

Aerospace and defence

Structural plate, bar and fasteners in Ti-6Al-4V (Grade 5), supplied to AMS specifications with heat-lot traceability and ultrasonic inspection where called for.

Ti-6Al-4V · AMS

Medical and dental

Grade 5 ELI and Grade 2 bar for implants and instruments, where biocompatibility and consistent mechanical properties matter more than the price per kilogram.

Gr.5 ELI · Gr.2

Power generation

Condenser tubing and turbine components where cooling water is saline or chlorinated. Grade 2 tube to ASTM B338 is the usual starting point.

Gr.2 · ASTM B338

Consumer and daily goods

Drinkware, cutlery and cookware in Grade 1 and Grade 2 — lightweight, corrosion-free and safe in contact with food and drink.

Gr.1 · Gr.2
