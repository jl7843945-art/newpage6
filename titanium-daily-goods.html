Which titanium grade is used?

The proposed range uses commercially pure titanium Gr.1 or Gr.2. Exact grade, wall thickness and finish are agreed in the order and confirmed before quoting.

Can capacity or branding be customised?

Yes. Capacity, dimensions, surface finish, laser marking and retail packaging can be specified in your enquiry; tooling and minimum quantities depend on the item and are confirmed per order.
