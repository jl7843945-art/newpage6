Titanium Products

Five product lines, one supplier: titanium plate and sheet, bar and rod, tube and pipe, fasteners, and precision forgings — produced to ASTM, GB and AMS standards.

MOQ 50 kg stock / 500 kg custom · Lead time 20 days · FOB / CIF / EXW · 30% T/T deposit, 70% before shipment

ALLOYTI sources and supplies titanium and titanium alloys, and supports industrial buyers across specification, inspection and documents. The range below covers the five product forms most often requested — each with its own page covering grades, standards, supply forms, inspection and documents.

Titanium Plate & Sheet

ASTM B265 · Gr.1 / Gr.2 / Gr.5 (TC4) · hot and cold rolled, coil, cut-to-size blanks.

Titanium Bar & Rod

ASTM B348 · Gr.2 / Gr.5 (TC4) / Gr.7 · round, peeled, turned, ground, cut to length.

Titanium Tube & Pipe

ASTM B338 / B861 / B862 · Gr.1 / Gr.2 / Gr.7 · seamless and welded, cut to length.

Titanium Fasteners

DIN / ISO / ASME · Gr.2 / Gr.5 (TC4) · bolts, nuts, screws, studs and washers.

Forgings & Machined Parts

ASTM B381 · TC4 (Gr.5), TA15, CP grades · discs, rings, blocks and CNC parts to drawing.

Machining and further processing

Bar and forgings can be supplied as machining stock or finished to drawing. The mills we work with cover straightening, coil slitting, heat treatment, turning and dimensional inspection, with discs and rings held in stock. Tolerances, surface finish and inspection scope are agreed as part of the order — see Quality & Service.
