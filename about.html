About ALLOYTI

A titanium supply partner working across aerospace, marine engineering, medical and new-energy applications — TA1, TC4 (Gr.5) and TA15, to ASTM, GB and AMS standards.

Across aerospace, marine engineering, medical and new-energy applications, titanium alloys combine strength, corrosion resistance, light weight and biocompatibility in a way no other structural metal does. That combination is the reason titanium keeps replacing stainless steel and nickel alloys in service where chloride, temperature or weight decide the outcome.

What ALLOYTI does

ALLOYTI focuses on the sourcing, supply and technical support of titanium and titanium alloys. We cover mainstream grades — TA1, TC4 (Gr.5 / Ti-6Al-4V) and TA15 — and deliver one-stop solutions in plate, bar, tube, fasteners and precision forgings to ASTM, GB and AMS standards. Our products are exported to North America, Europe, the Middle East and Southeast Asia.

Sourcing and processing

The mills we work with cover the steps that decide whether a titanium order arrives usable: coil slitting and cut-to-width processing, straightening of bar and tube, heat treatment to the required delivery condition, turning and machining of bar and forgings, and dimensional inspection before packing. Titanium discs and rings are held in stock so that repeat sizes can be shipped without waiting for a production slot at the mill. See our partner mills.

Quality & Compliance

  • EN 10204 3.1 — mill test certificate issued as standard.
  • Third-party inspection — SGS / TÜV / Bureau Veritas, on request.

Certificate copies and numbers are available on request. Any further approvals referenced in an enquiry are confirmed against current certificates before we commit to them.

The mills we work with operate independently audited quality management systems. The certificates referenced below belong to those mills, not to ALLOYTI. For each order we confirm the current scope, validity and product coverage of the specific mill before committing.

ISO 9001 — Quality management

Our partner mills maintain ISO 9001-certified quality management systems covering melting, rolling, forging and inspection. We match the certificate scope to the product form and grade in your enquiry.

AS9100 — Aerospace quality

For aerospace applications, material can be sourced from mills holding AS9100 certification, with the traceability and test reporting that entails. Availability is confirmed per order; we do not assume that one mill covers every product form.

PED 2014/68/EU — Pressure equipment

PED is an EU directive governing pressure equipment, not a company certificate. For EU pressure applications we provide the material documentation supporting the equipment manufacturer's conformity assessment: EN 10204 3.1 or 3.2 certificates, heat-number traceability and testing to the applicable standard. The equipment-level conformity assessment remains with the equipment manufacturer.

Copies of current mill certificates and their scopes are available on request; we verify them against the order before dispatch.

How we work with buyers

We supply more than material. Before an order is placed we confirm grade, standard, dimensions, tolerances, inspection scope and documents item by item — so the quotation you receive already reflects what can actually be produced and certified. After the order, we track packing, marking, inspection and dispatch milestones and keep you updated at the points that matter.

  • Grade selection advice when the service conditions are still open.
  • Process support — machining allowances, heat-treatment condition, surface finish.
  • Document support — EN 10204 certificates, packing lists and export paperwork.
  • Fast response — enquiries answered within one business day.

For quality procedures and the full document list, see Quality & Service. For grade and lead-time questions, see the sourcing FAQ.
