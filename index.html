ALLOYTI — Titanium Alloys Supply Partner & Supplier

Built on Titanium. Driven by Technology.

Titanium alloys combine low density (about 4.5 g/cm³) with high specific strength, excellent corrosion and heat resistance, and biocompatibility — key structural materials for aerospace, marine engineering, medical and new-energy applications. ALLOYTI supplies commercially pure titanium TA1/TA2 and alloys such as TC4 (Ti-6Al-4V), TA15 and TC11 in plate, bar, tube, forgings and fasteners, to ASTM, GB and AMS standards, with made-to-order processing and export delivery.

About ALLOYTI

Titanium & titanium alloys:
sourcing, supply and technical support.

Across aerospace, marine engineering, medical and new-energy applications, titanium alloys combine strength, corrosion resistance, light weight and biocompatibility. ALLOYTI focuses on the sourcing, supply and technical support of titanium and titanium alloys, covering mainstream grades such as TA1, TC4 (Gr.5) and TA15, and delivering one-stop solutions in plate, bar, tube and precision forgings to ASTM, GB and AMS standards. Our products are exported to North America, Europe, the Middle East and Southeast Asia. We supply more than material — we provide grade selection advice, process support and fast response.
