Terms of Sale

The commercial terms behind our quotations and orders — payment, delivery, inspection, claims and governing law.

Last updated: 2026-11-20

1. Scope

These terms apply to quotations, orders and deliveries of titanium products unless a signed contract or a confirmed proforma invoice states otherwise. They do not affect rights that cannot be excluded by law.

2. Quotations

Quotations are valid for 30 days from the date of issue unless stated otherwise, and are based on the grade, standard, size, quantity and destination given in the enquiry. A change to any of these may change the price and the lead time.

3. Orders and acceptance

An order becomes binding when we issue a written confirmation or a proforma invoice. The buyer is responsible for the accuracy of the specification, drawing and standard supplied; we confirm our interpretation in writing before production starts.

4. Prices and payment

Prices are in USD or EUR and exclude bank charges. Standard terms are a 30% T/T deposit with the order and 70% before shipment or against a copy of the bill of lading. Other terms are agreed in writing before production.

5. Delivery and Incoterms

Delivery follows the Incoterms 2020 rule named in the quotation — usually FOB, CIF or EXW. Lead time is about 20 days after the deposit and the drawing confirmation; it starts when both are received, not on the date of the enquiry.

6. Title and risk

Risk passes as defined by the agreed Incoterm. Title to the goods passes to the buyer when the invoice is paid in full.

7. Inspection and claims

Each lot is supplied with the mill certificate for its heat number. Claims for quantity, appearance or dimensions should reach us within 30 days of arrival, with photographs and packing details. Claims for concealed defects are handled under the warranty below.

8. Warranty

We warrant that the material conforms to the grade and standard stated in the order. If it does not, we will replace the affected material or credit its invoice value. The warranty does not cover wear, misuse, or defects caused by the buyer's machining, heat treatment or welding.

9. Force majeure

Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including natural events, war, sanctions, transport disruption or changes in export regulation. The affected party notifies the other promptly and the delivery date is extended accordingly.

10. Liability

Our liability for any claim is limited to the invoice value of the goods concerned. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, including lost production or lost profit.

11. Governing law and disputes

These terms are governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China. The parties will first attempt to settle any dispute by negotiation; if that fails, the dispute is referred to arbitration under the rules of CIETAC in Beijing.

12. Amendments and language

Amendments are valid only in writing and signed by both parties. This page is published in several languages; the English version prevails in case of conflict.

This page summarises our standard commercial terms. Where a signed contract or a confirmed proforma invoice differs, that document prevails.
