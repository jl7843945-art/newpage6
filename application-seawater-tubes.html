Seawater and desalination tube bundles

Grade 2 titanium tube to ASTM B338 / ASME SB338 for condenser and evaporator bundles, with Grade 2 tube sheet plate to ASTM B265. Seawater resistance, not strength, drives the material choice.

Material & specification facts

Material & grade

Grade 2 commercially pure titanium is selected for seawater corrosion resistance; Grade 2 plate is used for tube sheets.

Standards & forms

Tube to ASTM B338 / ASME SB338 and plate to ASTM B265; diameter, wall thickness and tube-sheet thickness follow the drawing.

Documents & inspection

EN 10204 3.1 mill certificate with heat number; UT or eddy-current inspection can be agreed in the order.

How to verify before dispatch

How to check: confirm the grade and standard on the mill certificate, match heat numbers to the packing list, and agree UT or eddy-current scope in the order.
